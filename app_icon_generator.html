🍪 Alfajores Collection - App Icon Options

Instructions:

  1. Choose your favorite icon design below
  2. Take a screenshot of the icon (make it square)
  3. Use an online tool like appicon.co or makeappicon.com to generate all required sizes
  4. Download the generated icon pack
  5. In Xcode: Assets.xcassets → AppIcon → drag the icons to corresponding slots
Required sizes: 1024×1024, 180×180, 120×120, 87×87, 80×80, 60×60, 58×58, 40×40
