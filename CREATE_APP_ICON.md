# Creating App Icon for Alfajores Collection

## Required Sizes for iOS App Icon

You need these exact sizes (PNG format, NO transparency):

### iPhone App Icon Sizes:
- **1024x1024** - App Store Connect
- **180x180** - iPhone @3x (iPhone 6 Plus, 6s Plus, 7 Plus, 8 Plus, X, XS, XS Max, XR, 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro Max, 12, 12 mini, 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max, 13, 14, 15 series)
- **120x120** - iPhone @2x (iPhone 4s, 5, 5c, 5s, 6, 6s, 7, 8, SE)
- **87x87** - iPhone @3x Settings
- **58x58** - iPhone @2x Settings
- **80x80** - iPhone @3x Spotlight
- **60x60** - iPhone @2x Spotlight
- **40x40** - iPhone @2x Notification
- **60x60** - iPhone @3x Notification

## Quick Creation Options:

### 1. Online Icon Generators:
- **Canva** - Has app icon templates
- **Figma** - Free design tool
- **App Icon Generator** websites

### 2. Use macOS Preview:
1. Create a 1024x1024 canvas
2. Add background color
3. Add text or shapes
4. Export as PNG

### 3. Simple Text Icon (Quickest):
- Background: #D2691E (chocolate color)
- Text: "🍪" or "A" in white
- Round corners applied

## Steps to Add to Xcode:

1. **Prepare all icon sizes** (use online resizer tools)
2. **Open Xcode project**
3. **Click on Assets.xcassets** in project navigator
4. **Click on AppIcon** 
5. **Drag each icon size** to corresponding slot
6. **Build and install** on your iPhone
