📱 How to Use:

Step 1: Take a screenshot of your favorite icon above (make it perfectly square)
Step 2: Go to makeappicon.com and upload your screenshot
Step 3: Download the iOS icon pack with all required sizes
Step 4: In Xcode → Assets.xcassets → AppIcon → drag icons to slots
Step 5: Build and install on your iPhone! 🎉

Tip: The cookie emoji will look great at any size and is instantly recognizable!
